# Causal queue evidence admission v1 — October 26

This is a separate offline engineering component for priority-zero executable-edge work. It does not register a new trading strategy, collect prices, reopen source v5, change frozen V15/V10, or authorize any trial. No market outcomes were evaluated with it. Freeze this component before any future prospective integration.

Inputs require exact ticker/epoch, independently current rules/lifecycle, verified fee semantics, a verified activation book/initial queue-ahead quantity, independently verified direction normalization, a verified clock bound of at least one second, and a complete trade tape for the activation-to-cancel-ack interval. The caller's flags are assertions requiring external evidence; this function does not authenticate them. No suitable full joined dataset currently exists in the closed source v5 archive, which contains zero public trades.

Only exact same-side, same-price public volume strictly between arrival+clock margin and cancel request−margin reduces the initial queue estimate. Displayed cancellations do not advance queue priority. Other-price prints are not converted to fills. Exact trade duplicates cannot double count; contradictory duplicates, epoch/ticker mismatch, receipt reversal and impossible clocks block. Clock-boundary/cancel races and partial depletion stay uncertain. Missing tape remains missing, not a no-fill record.

Even full modeled depletion returns only `conditional_queue_depletion`, with owned fills zero and execution, promotion and profitability false. Hidden priority, exchange matching behavior, actual acknowledgements, fees, exits and independent reconciliation remain separate unmet gates. Synthetic tests do not prove real queue priority or execution. The deliberately conservative same-level rule is an evidence screen, not a full exchange simulator or economic hypothesis. No economic sample/return threshold is replaced. The original 100-complete/40-independent-event/all-horizon cost-stress and confidence/untouched-validation gates remain unchanged.

Sixteen synthetic unit tests passed once. No deployment is appropriate yet: there is no prospective source/metadata/fee join, transport or worker integration. Next integration must preregister its own finite source budget, selection, timing, missing-data policy and untouched evidence before collecting or inspecting new outcomes. Existing physical disk capacity is not authority to reopen any exhausted experiment.
