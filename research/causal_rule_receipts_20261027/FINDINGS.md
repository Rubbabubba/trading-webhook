# Causal rule receipt component, October 27

Separate offline component; no worker import/deployment, order interface, new collection protocol, price download or strategy change. The frozen source v5 and comparison v4 stay closed. V4 has now also reached its original 72-hour admission deadline, not a fresh formula arithmetic failure. Original missing outcome remains missing.

The prior queue component accepted proof flags supplied by a caller; these are not authentication. This new component checks the actual existing rule-archive version/receipt hashes, exact ticker/version joins, causal request/response order, first-observed time, lifecycle timezone and a fixed 30-second metadata age policy. Later receipts cannot backdate an earlier decision. It remains a narrow integrity screen: hashes cannot authenticate a venue, verify fee semantics, assure status between observations, or establish owned queue priority. Even synthetic valid inputs leave authentication, fees, queue, execution and promotion false.

Thirteen synthetic cases passed once, including altered rules/clocks, future/stale receipts, nonfinite/boolean clocks, identity mismatches, invalid lifecycle timezones, widened-policy rejection and missing current status.

The frozen v1 archive's metadata projection does not retain `status`. That is an explicit missing input for execution-readiness assessment; it does not invalidate its original rule-vintage archival purpose. Three fresh authentic rows were downloaded once through the authorized read-only Render shell. Their original hashes/bytes and separate local diagnostic are retained. The audit anchors at the book-receipt clock only; this is not an actual historical strategy decision. No original archive fields are patched or status invented. New prospective integration needs a separately versioned current-status/fee receipt with source provenance, lifecycle/clock boundaries and finite preregistration. Old hashes and frozen strategies remain intact. No price outcome is inspected or scored by this component.
