The user authorized the simulation layer on September 10 after confirming that the expansion had only been recording data. This is a separately versioned exploratory experiment. Mathematical and software checks do not establish predictive calibration, realistic execution under all conditions, or profitable trading. The old college, MLB and NFL baselines and both capture programs remain unchanged.

Matches require exact normalized full/display participant names, explicit inspected aliases, a unique same-date Kalshi event, and the expected binary outcomes. Soccer includes three separate home/draw/away YES contracts, each with regulation-plus-stoppage rules. All mapped configurations and rule evidence become immutable database rows. Changed mappings pause that game for review.

## Experimental models

- College football: ESPN's reported win probability, linked to the latest compatible drive play, team identities, matching score and actual play timestamp within 90 seconds. No play timestamps are repaired. Stale/missing probabilities block entries.
- Soccer: independent Poisson remaining goals, with separate home/draw/away probabilities. Fit the two scoring rates to a prospectively observed pregame market probability vector; reject a fit with more than four percentage points maximum error. Update from elapsed clock and goals. Stop new entries at 90 minutes, and block a reported red card. Constant scoring rates omit tactical, lineup, injury and unreported red-card effects. A good pregame fit is not an independent predictive validation.
- MLB: a Poisson remaining-runs approximation using outs, inning, half, score and a small fixed baserunner allowance. A 4.5-run-per-team reference assumption is scaled to a prospectively observed pregame market probability; it is an experimental constant, not an estimated current league statistic. The official MLB game ID and live state must match, ESPN and MLB scores must agree, and the reference play timestamp must be within 90 seconds. Extra innings, three-out transitions and walkoff transitions block entries. Tied projected regulation scores are split 50/50. Pitcher, batter, count, lineup and detailed run-expectancy effects are omitted.
- Tennis: a recursive game/set/match probability approximation, best of five for US Open men's singles and best of three for women's singles. Fit a constant game-win probability to a prospective pregame market probability. Update from completed sets and current games. The model ignores serving player and within-game points, and approximates tiebreak win probability by game-win probability. These substantial limitations make this a research baseline, not a validated tennis model. Unchanged game/set state beyond 180 seconds blocks entries, even if transport is healthy.

For soccer, MLB and tennis, freeze the first eligible anchor observed within two hours before scheduled start. Do not backfill historical pregame quotes after the game starts. These models are market-anchored, not independent estimates that inherently beat the market. Their larger entry margins are experimental uncertainty allowances, not statistical confidence intervals.

## Simulated accounts and execution

Each game has two independent hypothetical $1,000 accounts, with five- and fifteen-minute maximum intended holding periods. Do not add their balances as if they were one funded portfolio. Entry limits: $10 including costs, ten contracts, one open position, five entries per account, and a five-minute cooldown after exit. A $20 marked account loss halts entries; a 25% position loss stages an exit. Liquidity failures or price gaps can exceed intended limits.

Entry requires a valid model signal, fresh bid/ask depth, spread at most four cents, and at most 15% immediate modeled liquidation loss. Required cost-adjusted probability margins are four percentage points for college football, six for soccer, and eight for MLB and tennis. There is no trade quota. Save specific rejection reasons when a game produces no trades.

Taker fees use the observed Kalshi quadratic fee multiplier, rounded upward to whole cents conservatively. The engine adds one cent per contract per transaction as slippage and reserves exit fees/slippage in entry evaluation. Unknown/stale fee metadata blocks fills. Maker rebates and passive fills are not assumed. Public fee reference: https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf .

Orders are simulated only after another observation of the selected book at least ten seconds after staging and within 180 seconds. Entries use the worse of the staged and confirming asks, reject more than a two-cent worsening and recheck costs/edge/depth. Exits use the worse bid and sufficient displayed size. No queue priority or partial fills are assumed. Holding-limit, model-value, loss, completion and persistent bad-feed conditions can stage exits. All fills remain approximations from sampled books, not proof of attainable live execution. Public order-book reference: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/orderbook_responses .

Final settlement requires a public Kalshi finalized yes/no result with the same market/event and saved rules. No score-derived or deadline-derived payouts. Report unresolved positions separately and mark unavailable liquidation equity as unknown. Preserve ledgers on restart, and make settlement idempotent.

## Inputs, reports and operation

The worker reads the existing raw SQLite archives read-only. It verifies archived bytes against their SHA-256 and retains exact source archive, row ID, hash and transport metadata with each observation. No recorded historical sample is retroactively traded. Books and game feeds older than 120 seconds, HTTP cache age over 20 seconds, or failed transport block entries. The original collection cadence is approximately 20 seconds for MLB and one minute for expansion sports, plus any overruns. This is not a low-latency streaming bot.

Launch/resume with tools/start_sports_paper_research.ps1. The frozen protocol is configs/sports_paper_research_20260910/manifest.json. Persistent ledger: sports_paper/research_20260910/paper.sqlite3. Status: status.json. Errors: paper.stderr.log. Windows task Codex Sports Paper Research Recovery 20260910 restarts a stopped worker every five minutes and at logon, bounded through September 18 at 12:00 UTC. The signed-in computer must remain awake. Root PAUSE or a league_eventid.PAUSE file pauses decisions; dependent capture PAUSE files are also respected. Existing positions may settle while paused.

Each mapped game receives games/<league_eventid>/report.md and report.json, refreshed periodically, at trades and at completion/deadline. Reports show both holding accounts, trades, realized net P&L, modeled fees/slippage, drawdown, open positions, exclusions and trade examples. Zero entries are not a success. Final review should compare the models with market probabilities using held-out games, inspect latency and missing intervals, and separate trading P&L from model calibration. Do not change this version's settings in response to early results.
