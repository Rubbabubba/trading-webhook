# Official release research capture

The first open-ended Life OS idea, **Official-Release Stale Quotes v1**, requires data outside the price-bin strategy factory. This probe starts prospective acquisition on the existing Kalshi **Demo** worker. It has no order route and cannot declare a profitable strategy.

The worker reads the [BEA machine-readable release calendar](https://apps.bea.gov/API/signup/release_dates.json) at most once per six hours. It currently recognizes Gross Domestic Product and Personal Income and Outlays. The schedule is a plan, not evidence that a release was actually published. The collector uses the maker's existing market catalog and records a bounded watchlist only when the contract is active, binary, its rule text names BEA, its ticker belongs to a mapped series, and the contract closes after the scheduled release but within two days. It records the contract rule hash and flags close times more than two hours after the schedule for review. A one-day discrepancy in the current GDP contract is why this check is explicit.

During the ten minutes before and after a scheduled release, the collector may fetch at most two fresh Demo order books per normal worker cycle. It keeps five price and size levels per side plus request and observation timestamps. The existing Demo market client enforces freshness and rate limits. [Kalshi's order-book documentation](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-orderbook) explains that it exposes bids on both sides; an ask must be inferred from the opposing bid. These snapshots are **not** subsecond, filled, or live-market evidence.

The Life OS Kalshi page shows calendar freshness, watchlist counts, quote counts, and timing-review counts. The idea stays in `capability_needed`. Before it can become a scored shadow strategy, add a versioned contract-rule-to-official-value mapping, publication-time source capture with vintage controls, a frozen decision algorithm, fee and depth-aware fill modeling, and prospective out-of-sample release events. A Demo execution trial and the existing promotion dossier remain separate later gates.
